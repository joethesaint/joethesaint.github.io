Reference & concept notes

Body dimensions follow the published manufacturer specification. The reel, display, rocker, side buttons and connector layout are interpreted from product photos and the hardware guide. Small dimensions, engraving, rear details and internal construction are approximate. This independent visual study is not affiliated with Teenage Engineering.

Record, Play, Memo and scrubbing change the simulated display and reel. No microphone is accessed and no audio is recorded or stored. Hairline shows external geometry; it does not reveal an invented internal assembly.
