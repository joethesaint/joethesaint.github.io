Sweeping parameters: plateau or spike?

MT5's optimizer runs a grid of settings. Used to pick the best cell it is data mining: every cell is a trial and the bar rises. Used as a robustness check it is valuable: keep the declared setting and look at its neighbours. A real edge sits on a plateau, a fitted one on a spike. Monte Carlo answers a different question (luck), so the two are complementary.

A plateau test keeps the declared setting, so it adds no new trials to the count. It can only reject (a spike), never promote a different setting.
